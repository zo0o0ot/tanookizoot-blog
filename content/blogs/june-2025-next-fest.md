## 1. [Baki Hanma Blood Arena](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3469230/Baki_Hanma_Blood_Arena/?curator_clanid=42310171)

![Baki Hanma Blood Arena](/images/reviews/baki-hanma-blood-arena.jpg)

I'd really like for there to be more spiritual successors to the Punch Out Franchise, since the last official installment was a Nintendo Wii game. It looks like Baki Hanma Blood Arena is throwing its hat into the ring as a possible successor to the title. It appears to be an anime style 2D fighting game with very similar thematic elements to Punch Out. It's certainly a promising demo, and I'm wishlisting it in the hopes of reliving more Punch Out glory. As a note, Purple Tree, the developer, also made another Punch Out style game called Thunder Ray that I have not played but the trailer looks really cool.

## 2. [Ball x Pit](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2062430/BALL_x_PIT/)

![Ball x Pit](/images/reviews/ball-x-pit.jpg)

Ball x Pit is a new take on the old school Breakout or brick breaking genre. It adds some modern elements to an old game type and keeps a consistent theme by referring to the defeated kingdom of "Ballbalon" and the goal of restoring the kingdom to glory. ## 3. [Chill with You : Lo-Fi Story](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3548580/Chill_with_You__LoFi_Story/)

![Chill with You : Lo-Fi Story](/images/reviews/chill-with-you-lo-fi-story.jpg)

Chill with you appears to be halfway between a productivity app and a narrative game. Generally, the vibe is similar to Spirit City: Lofi Sessions, but the avatar is a different character that you hang out with instead of an avatar of yourself. There are many productivity similarities, but instead of collecting being the main goal, it appears that getting to know this random girl with glasses is the goal. It looks like the game is being developed in Japanese, as some of the localization still needs some polish. Overall, it's a very promising demo and I will be wishlisting it to follow along.
